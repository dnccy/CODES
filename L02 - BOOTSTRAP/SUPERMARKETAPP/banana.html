Banana

$1.80

Creamy, naturally sweet bananas are a wholesome fruit packed with potassium, Vitamin B6, and fiber. Their smooth texture and mild flavor make them perfect for breakfast, smoothies, or a quick energy boost throughout the day.

    Origin: First domesticated in Papua New Guinea and Southeast Asia, later spread worldwide
    Taste profile: Sweet, creamy, mild
    Uses: Ideal for eating fresh, blending into smoothies, baking banana bread, or frying plantains
    Storage: Best kept at room temperature; refrigerate to slow ripening (skin may darken but fruit stays fresh)
