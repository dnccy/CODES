Juicy, bite‑sized grapes are a refreshing snack rich in antioxidants and Vitamin K. Available in green, red, and purple varieties, they’re perfect for eating fresh, drying into raisins, or pressing into juice and wine.

    Origin: Cultivated for over 6,000 years in the Transcaucasus region, later spread across the Mediterranean and worldwide
    Taste profile: Sweet, juicy, sometimes slightly tart
    Uses: Great for snacking, fruit salads, raisins, jams, jellies, wine production
    Storage: Keep refrigerated to maintain firmness and freshness; consume within 1–2 weeks
